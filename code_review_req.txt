Manual review on JWT Diagnostics.
I have hooked into the `JwtBearerEvents` in `Program.cs` to add precise verbose logging via `ILogger` for each step of the JWT authentication process (`OnMessageReceived`, `OnTokenValidated`, `OnAuthenticationFailed`, and `OnChallenge`). This will dump the exact reason for the 401 directly into the terminal.
Additionally, I've placed trace logs at the very top of `GetMisPermisos()` to dump incoming authorization headers and claim counts.
The solution compiles and is ready for the user to execute.
